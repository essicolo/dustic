# How to Clear PWA Cache and See Latest Updates

If you're seeing an old version of Dustic after an update, follow these steps to clear the cache.

## iOS (Safari/iPhone/iPad)

### Method 1: Hard Refresh (In Browser)
1. Open Dustic in Safari
2. Tap the **Address bar** at the top
3. Tap the **Refresh button** (circular arrow icon)
4. **Force reload**: Pull down the page and hold for a few seconds before releasing

### Method 2: Clear Website Data
1. Open **Settings** app on your device
2. Scroll down and tap **Safari**
3. Scroll down to tap **Advanced**
4. Tap **Website Data**
5. In the search box, type **"dustic"**
6. Swipe left on the dustic.app entry
7. Tap **Delete**
8. Go back to Safari and reload dustic.app

### Method 3: Reinstall PWA (If Installed as App)
1. **Delete** the Dustic app from your home screen (long press → Remove App → Delete App)
2. Open Safari and go to dustic.app
3. Follow Method 2 above to clear website data
4. In Safari, tap the **Share button** (square with arrow pointing up)
5. Scroll down and tap **"Add to Home Screen"**
6. Tap **"Add"**

## Android (Chrome/Firefox)

### Method 1: Hard Refresh (In Browser)
1. Open Dustic in Chrome/Firefox
2. Tap the **menu (three dots)** in the top-right
3. Tap **Settings**
4. Tap **Site settings**
5. Find and tap **dustic.app**
6. Tap **Clear & reset**
7. Go back and reload the page

### Method 2: Clear App Data (If Installed)
1. Open **Settings** on your Android device
2. Tap **Apps** (or **Applications**)
3. Find and tap **Dustic**
4. Tap **Storage**
5. Tap **Clear storage** or **Clear data**
6. Tap **Clear cache**
7. Reopen Dustic

### Method 3: Reinstall PWA
1. Go to **Settings** → **Apps**
2. Find **Dustic** and tap **Uninstall**
3. Open Chrome and go to dustic.app
4. Tap the **menu (three dots)**
5. Tap **"Install app"** or **"Add to Home screen"**

## Desktop (Chrome/Edge/Firefox)

### Chrome/Edge:
1. Open dustic.app in browser
2. Press **Ctrl+Shift+Delete** (Windows/Linux) or **Cmd+Shift+Delete** (Mac)
3. Select **"Cached images and files"**
4. Make sure time range is set to **"All time"**
5. Click **Clear data**
6. Reload the page with **Ctrl+F5** (Windows/Linux) or **Cmd+Shift+R** (Mac)

### Firefox:
1. Open dustic.app
2. Press **Ctrl+Shift+Delete** (Windows/Linux) or **Cmd+Shift+Delete** (Mac)
3. Select **"Cache"**
4. Click **Clear Now**
5. Reload with **Ctrl+Shift+R** (Windows/Linux) or **Cmd+Shift+R** (Mac)

## How to Know You Have the Latest Version

After clearing cache and reloading:
1. Click the **hamburger menu** (☰) in the top-left
2. Scroll down and click **About**
3. Check the **Version** section at the bottom
4. It should show the current version (check GitHub releases for the latest)

## Future Updates

Once you have version **0.0.2 or higher**, Dustic will automatically notify you when updates are available! You'll see a notification at the top of the screen:

**"Update Available - A new version of Dustic is available"**

Simply click **"Update Now"** and the app will refresh with the latest version while keeping all your downloaded music and settings.

## What Gets Preserved During Updates

✅ **Preserved (Never Deleted):**
- Downloaded music files
- Favorites and playlists
- Listening history
- User profile and settings
- Offline track metadata

✅ **Updated:**
- App code (HTML, JavaScript, CSS)
- UI improvements and bug fixes
